YT Manager

A personal desktop dashboard for managing several YouTube channels in one place.

WHAT IT DOES

  • Connects the owner's YouTube channels from several Google accounts via Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0).
  • Shows channel statistics and YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, subscribers, estimated revenue) on one screen.
  • Opens YouTube Studio for each channel in a separate browser profile.
  • Planned: uploading videos and replying to comments on the owner's channels.

WHO IT IS FOR

YT Manager is a private tool used only by its developer to manage his own YouTube channels. It is not sold or distributed to the public.

YOUR DATA

All data stays on the user's own computer. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. Read the full Privacy Policy.
