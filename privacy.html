Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

YT Manager ("the app") is a desktop application for Windows used by its developer to manage his own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests access to:

  • your Google account email address (to label which account a channel belongs to);
  • YouTube channel information and statistics (YouTube Data API);
  • YouTube Analytics and revenue reports for your channels (YouTube Analytics API);
  • permission to upload videos and manage comments on your own channels.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to display your channel statistics and reports inside the app and to perform actions you start yourself (such as opening YouTube Studio, uploading a video or replying to a comment). The app does not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Where the data is stored

All data is stored only on your own computer. Access tokens are encrypted with the Windows data protection system. The app has no server: no data is sent to the developer or to any third party. The app communicates only with Google's own services (accounts.google.com, googleapis.com).

4. Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

YT Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Remove a channel in the app — its stored data and token are deleted from your computer.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the folder %APPDATA%\yt-manager to remove all locally stored data.
